**Levelling to 6★ level 60 multiplies HP, ATK and DEF by about 10. Full Awakening then adds roughly a quarter more ATK, plus large flat bonuses to Instinct, speed and more.**

- Ascending raises stars and needs level = stars × 10, up to level 60 at 6★. Levelling and ascending are one road.
- Awakening comes in six levels, and each needs that many stars: the 6th needs 6★.
- Awakening percentages are of the Invoker's 1★ level 1 stats, so they add far less than the raw numbers suggest: +300% ATK becomes about +26% at level 60.
- Awakening also unlocks accessory slots, and its bonuses are the same at every rarity; only the cost goes up.

What each type of Awakening gives: [What each Awakening Sign gives](/blog/awakening-signs).

Examples: ![Almea](hero:) (Epic, Melee) and ![Duchess Kanraku](hero:) (Legendary, Ranged). Costs: [Ascension and Awakening](/ascension).

## Levelling: first day vs 6★ level 60

Epics start at 4★, Legendaries at 5★.

| | Start | 6★ level 60 | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![Almea](hero:) HP | 655 | 6,532 | ×10.0 |
| ATK | 75 | 745 | ×9.9 |
| DEF | 61 | 607 | ×10.0 |
| ![Duchess Kanraku](hero:) HP | 684 | 6,532 | ×9.5 |
| ATK | 106 | 1,008 | ×9.5 |
| DEF | 78 | 745 | ×9.6 |

Start is level 1 at the starting star. Crit, speed and the other stats don't change with level or stars.
