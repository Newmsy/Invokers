**Levelling to 6★ level 60 multiplies HP, ATK and DEF by about 10. Full Awakening then adds roughly +27% ATK and +12% HP, plus large boosts to Instinct, speed and other ratings.**

- Ascending raises stars and needs level = stars × 10, up to level 60 at 6★. Levelling and ascending are one road.
- Awakening comes in six levels, and each needs that many stars: the 6th needs 6★.
- Awakening's % bonuses are a share of the Invoker's 1★ level 1 stats, so at level 60 they're worth about a tenth of what the number says. Full Awakening's ATK (+26 to 28%) is less than the last star and ten levels (+40%).
- Its flat bonuses (Instinct, speed, ACC, Mana Gen) are where Awakening shines: Instinct goes from 5 to 41.
- Awakening also unlocks accessory slots, and its bonuses are the same at every rarity; only the cost goes up.

Examples: ![Almea](hero:) (Epic, Melee) and ![Duchess Kanraku](hero:) (Legendary, Ranged). Costs: [Ascension and Awakening](/ascension).

Epics start at 4★, Legendaries at 5★.

| ![Almea](hero:) HP | 655 | 6,532 | ×10.0 |
| ATK | 75 | 745 | ×9.9 |
| DEF | 61 | 607 | ×10.0 |
| ![Duchess Kanraku](hero:) HP | 684 | 6,532 | ×9.5 |
| ATK | 106 | 1,008 | ×9.5 |
| DEF | 78 | 745 | ×9.6 |

Start is level 1 at the starting star. Crit, speed and the other ratings don't change with level or stars.
