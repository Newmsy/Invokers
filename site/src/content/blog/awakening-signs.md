**Each Invoker awakens along one of eight Signs, and the Sign decides what full Awakening gives.**

- **Attackers:** Sign of Duthra (+29% ATK), Temero (+28%) and Vess (+26%).
- **Tanks:** Sign of Magnar (+23% DEF) and Actia (+21% DEF), with the most RES and Instinct.
- **Supports:** Sign of Pirea (+26% HP) and Kalibar (+21% HP, +19% ATK).
- Every Sign adds +3s Titan Invokation Time. The bonuses are the same at every rarity.

How this compares with levelling: [Levelling vs Awakening](/blog/levelling-vs-awakening). Costs: [Ascension and Awakening](/ascension).
