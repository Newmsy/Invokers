**Every trophy costs the same: 330 ![Bronze](item:Bronze%20Trophy), 960 ![Silver](item:Silver%20Trophy) and 1,700 ![Gold](item:Gold%20Trophy) Trophies to take it from 0 to level 15.**

- Levels 1–5 cost Bronze, 6–10 Silver, 11–15 Gold.
- A full room (all 12 trophies, one element) costs **3,960 Bronze, 11,520 Silver and 20,400 Gold**.
- All six rooms: **23,760 Bronze, 69,120 Silver and 122,400 Gold** (estimated, assuming every room has all 12 trophies).
- Cheapest bonus in the room: level 1 of anything, 20 Bronze.

## In Gold Trophies

A Gold Trophy is worth 2 Silver, and a Silver is worth 2 Bronze. Counting everything in Gold:

| | Gold Trophies |
|---|---|
| One trophy, 0 → 15 | 2,262.5 |
| One room (12 trophies) | 27,150 |
| All six rooms (estimated) | 162,900 |

Gold levels (11–15) are 75% of the total cost.

Plan your own upgrades on the [Trophy Room](/trophies) page.

## What level 15 gives

Each bonus applies to every Invoker of that room's element, in every battle.

| Trophy | Per level | Level 15 |
|---|---|---|
| ATK, DEF, HP | +2% | +30% |
| Crit Rate | +1% | +15% |
| Crit DMG | +2% (1–5), +3% (6–15) | +40% |
| ACC, RES | +5 (1–10), +10 (11–15) | +100 |
| Combo SPD, Skill SPD, Skill RECOV, Mana Gen, Instinct | +4 | +60 |

**Gold levels are the best value for ACC, RES and Crit DMG**, because their bonus per level goes up. ATK, DEF and HP give the same +2% at every level, so each Gold level costs much more for the same gain.

## Where to start

1. Take every trophy in your main element's room to level 5. It costs Bronze only (3,960 for the room) and gives +10% ATK, DEF and HP.
2. Spend Silver on ATK, HP and DEF next, then Crit DMG.
3. Spend Gold on ACC, RES and Crit DMG first, where each Gold level gives more than the levels before it. Boss Resistance climbs from 50 to 635 across the stages: see *How bosses get harder, stage by stage*.

Pick your room with *The roster by the numbers*: Fire has the most Invokers (50).
