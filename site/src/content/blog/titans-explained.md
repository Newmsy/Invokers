**Bind a Titan from the Invoker's own faction. Only ![Litria](titan:) works with everyone.**

- A Titan's bonus to its bound Invoker **doubles** from 0 to 6 Empowerment.
- Boss-mode Titans give a bigger bonus in boss fights and a smaller one elsewhere. For bosses, take them.
- Invokation lasts **12 seconds** (15 with full Awakening) and comes back **180 seconds** after it ends.

## Binding

- One Titan per Invoker, and no sharing within a battle. Auto-pick takes your highest-Power Titan of the right faction, not the best bonus.
- Titans have no element. They take the bound Invoker's.
- Per faction: Ironbands and Eldrin have 6 Titans; Nocturna and Stonehearts 5; Cambion 4; Lightseekers, Freeborn and Magi 3; Beastbloods 2.

## In battle

- Every Invoker fills one shared **Titan Mana** pool. Extra mana isn't wasted.
- Only one Titan can be out at a time. It hits with an Invokation effect on arrival and an end effect (usually a buff or heal) when it leaves.
- Skill cooldowns keep running while transformed, and buffs and debuffs carry over. Titans don't build mana.
- Every Awakening Sign adds **+3s** Invokation time in total (+1s at 2★, 4★ and 6★).

## Upgrading

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Level | XP potions only, never battles. Max level 60 at 6★. |
| Ascension (yellow stars) | Current ★ count of same-★ Titans or Dragonsouls, e.g. three 3★ for 3★ → 4★ |
| Empowerment (purple stars) | Each step: 1 Titanheart (Rare), 2 Titanhearts (Epic), 1 Elder Titanheart (Legendary), or 1 duplicate. Six steps to max. |
| Skills | Rare, Epic and Legendary Prisms. Empowerment, not Prisms, raises the bonus. |

Full Empowerment costs 6 Titanhearts for a Rare, 12 for an Epic or 6 Elder Titanhearts for a Legendary.
