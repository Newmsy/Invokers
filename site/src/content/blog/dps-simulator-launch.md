**The [DPS simulator](/dps-simulator) is live.** It estimates how much damage per second an Invoker deals to [Ulgorim](/bosses/ulgorim), which substat would add the most, and which gear from your box gives the most damage.

Find it under **Tools** in the menu, or press **Simulate DPS** on any Invoker's page.

![The DPS simulator's Invoker picker, sorted by highest DPS](/blog/dps-simulator-picker.jpg)

## How to use it

1. **Pick an Invoker** from the portrait grid. Search by name, and sort by highest DPS, stars and level, rarity or name. Choose **My box** to use your Invokers' own level, stars, Awakening, skills and gear, or **Any Invoker** for 6★ level 60, fully awakened, with no gear.
2. **Pick a mode.** **PvE** starts with every skill ready over a one-minute fight. **Arena** starts skills at half cooldown, as real Arena battles do, over a 25-second fight (about how long a top-level Arena fight lasts).
3. **Pick an Ulgorim stage:** 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16 or 20. Higher stages have more DEF and RES, so DPS drops and fewer debuffs land.

No box yet? [Import it on My box](/my-box). It stays in your browser.

## What it shows

- **Estimated DPS**, plus total damage over the fight.
- **Stats used:** the final ATK, crit, ACC and other stats behind the number.
- **Where the damage comes from:** Combo, Skills 1–3 and the Ultimate, with uses, damage and share of the total.

![Estimated DPS and where the damage comes from for Ozmol the Vulcanist at Ulgorim stage 16](/blog/dps-simulator-results.jpg)

- **Debuffs on the boss:** each debuff and control effect the Invoker's skills cast, the chance it lands against the stage's RES, and how many land in the fight. Burn and Bleed only deal damage when they land, so ACC matters.

![With only 23 ACC, none of these debuffs land on Ulgorim stage 16](/blog/dps-simulator-debuffs.jpg)

- **What one more substat roll is worth:** the DPS one normal (B grade) 5★ roll of each stat would add. Use it to decide which substats to keep and upgrade.
- **What levelling a skill is worth** (your box only): the DPS from +1 level and from maxing each skill that isn't maxed yet.

## Best gear from your box

Pick an Invoker from your box and a **Best gear from your box** section appears. For each slot it shows what the Invoker wears now, the best piece to swap in, and the **DPS gain** of that swap.

- Each swap's gain is what the piece adds on top of the other suggested pieces, so set bonuses count.
- **Set bonuses now** and **Set bonuses with the best gear** show which sets you keep, gain or lose.
- An **Equipped** badge names who is wearing a piece. Untick **Use gear other Invokers are wearing** to leave their gear alone.
- Accessories only come from the Invoker's own faction.
- Accessory slots open with Awakening: Amulet from the start, Earrings at 2, Banner at 4, Talisman at 5, Flasks at 6.

## What to keep in mind

All numbers are **estimated**. Each fight assumes:

- one target, with the buffs an Invoker casts on itself (or on allies around it) but none from other Invokers,
- debuffs that land don't change damage (except Burn and Bleed),
- average crits rather than lucky ones,
- Burn and Bleed at their per-tick limit.

Element advantage counts. Each Ulgorim stage has an element, so an Invoker with advantage deals +30% damage and lands debuffs more often. An Invoker at a disadvantage deals −30% damage and has 50 points less Crit Rate. Light and Dark Invokers deal +15% damage against every stage.

Compare Invokers and gear against each other rather than treating the number as exact. The estimates will sharpen as more battle rules are confirmed.
