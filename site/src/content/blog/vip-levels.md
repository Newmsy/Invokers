**Logins alone reach VIP 10 in about 400 days. Past that, VIP is bought.** VIP 25 takes **10,043,600 points**, about **$100,436 (£100,436)** of Shop purchases.

- **Purchases:** 100 VIP points per $1 or £1 spent in the Shop. Gem purchases don't count.
- **Logins:** 50 points on day 1 of a login streak, rising to **300 a day** from day 17.
- **VIP 10 is the sweet spot.** Gold and XP boosts max out there (+50%), and it costs $1,186 if you buy it.
- Boosts only work **while VIP is active**. The daily VIP chest is free either way.

- **Gold, XP and Memorium:** extra from battles. XP means XP Potions.
- **Rune drops:** your chance of a Rune drop in Campaign battles is multiplied by this.
- **Battle stats:** bonus HP, ATK and DEF in Campaign and boss battles.
- **Free VIP days:** active VIP days you get on reaching the level.

## Other perks

- **VIP 2:** a VIP badge in chat.
- **VIP 8:** Daily Quests complete themselves as soon as you finish them.
- **VIP 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21 and 25:** a new avatar frame each.

## Keeping VIP active

| VIP time | ![Gems](item:) Cost |

| 1 day | 300 |

The 30-day pass is the best value: 100 Gems a day, against 300 for a single day.

## The daily VIP chest

One free reward a day, better at each level:

- **VIP 1:** ![Gold](item:) 10K (5%), ![Memorium](item:) 100 (10%), ![Gems](item:) 5 (20%), ![Titan Spark](item:) 20 (15%), Runes, Essences, ![Multi-Battle Tokens](item:) and a 1★ Echo.
- **VIP 5:** ![Rare Codex](item:) and ![Rare Prism](item:) appear (1% each).
- **VIP 9:** ![Epic Codex](item:) and ![Epic Prism](item:) appear, and Echoes become 3★.
- **VIP 10:** Gems jump to 70 or more (10% chance), and Memorium reaches 1,000.
- **VIP 13:** ![Legendary Codex](item:) and ![Legendary Prism](item:) appear (1% each, 6% at VIP 25). Memorium peaks at 33%. Runes and Essences stop.

Gold goes up by 10K every level, to 250K at VIP 25. Full odds by level: [VIP chests](/blog/chest-odds#vip-chests).

These are odds over many chests, not a forecast of your next one.
