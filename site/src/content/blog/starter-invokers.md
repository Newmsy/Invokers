**Take Selisa if unsure.** She ranks highest across our tier lists and her DEF Down helps every team.

- All five are Rare, so they share one Tolerance at 50% and the same upgrade costs.
- Pick by the job, not the damage: Selisa debuffs, Dannos and Silanth clear waves, Kalix burns, Malgar launches.
- A built starter beats an unbuilt Legendary. Spend your first [Codexes](/skill-costs) here.

## What they do at max skill level

- **Selisa:** Biting Shadow puts **35% DEF Down** for 9s on one target. Phantom Claws slows 8 enemies by 60% for 14s. Her Ultimate stuns 8 enemies for 4s.
- **Dannos:** Smitebringer places **10% Weaken** for 9s. His Ultimate hits 8 enemies 8 times and gets stronger the more HP he has.
- **Silanth:** Bloodpetal Harvest bleeds 8 enemies for **1.5% max HP** every 2s for 8s (capped at 300% ATK a tick). Her Ultimate gains 15% per debuff on the target, up to +75%.
- **Kalix:** Breath of the Drake burns 8 enemies for **1.25% max HP** a tick for 6s. His Ultimate instantly ticks up to 3 Burns. Might of Constraint adds 30% ATK Down.
- **Malgar:** The Stinger launches 8 enemies for 2s. His Ultimate gives him **+20% Combo SPD** for 10s.

Every debuff needs Accuracy to land. Ultimates start at 900 mana (Selisa and Kalix 1,100) and get cheaper as you upgrade them. Read [How combat works](/blog/combat-basics) before stage 10 of any boss.

## Building them

- **Silanth, Kalix:** ATK and ACC. Their Bleeds and Burns ignore DEF and scale with ATK, so skip Crit.
- **Selisa:** ACC first so her debuffs land, then ATK.
- **Dannos, Malgar:** Crit Rate to 75-80%, then Crit DMG and ACC.
- Everyone: Awakening can't go past an Invoker's Ascension stars, so raise stars first ([Ascension](/ascension)).
