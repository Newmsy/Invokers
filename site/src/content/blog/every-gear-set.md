There are **31 gear sets**, each with a bonus at 3 pieces, 5 pieces or both. None has an 8-piece bonus. The 5-piece bonus adds to the 3-piece one, so 5 pieces give both.

- Tolerance sets give the best all-round 5-piece bonus, whichever corruption they're named for.
- Vampire's skill lifesteal is 6%, not the 5% its description says.
- Small rating sets are worth more than they look on Invokers with little of that stat.

## The combination sets

A full set gives both columns: a full Predator set is +10% ATK and +40% Crit DMG.

| Set | 3 pieces | 5 pieces add |
|---|---|---|
| ![Predator](set:) | +10% ATK | +40% Crit DMG |
| ![Vampire](set:) | +10% ATK | Lifesteal: 6% of skill damage, 1% of combo damage |
| ![Bulwark](set:) | +75 RES | +30% HP |
| ![Celerity](set:) | +45 Mana Gen | +45 Skill Recovery |
| ![Paragon](set:) | +15% heal and shield size | Allies move faster out of combat |
| ![Voodoo](set:) | Combo attacks 3 and 5 shorten 1 enemy buff | A stronger version of the same effect |
| ![Guardian](set:) | +20% HP | All allies get a shield worth 20% of the wearer's max HP for 12 seconds |
| ![Spellwall](set:) | Willpower and Immunity buffs at the start of battle | +10% HP, ATK and DEF |

- **Vampire** says 5% of skill damage but heals **6%**. The 1% combo figure matches.
- **Voodoo** cuts a buff by up to 3 seconds per hit.
- Guardian's shield and Spellwall's buffs are effects, so they don't show on your stat screen.

## The twelve Tolerance sets

One per boss corruption: ![Crush](set:crush-tolerance), ![Manashock](set:manashock-tolerance), ![Frost](set:frost-tolerance), ![Daze](set:daze-tolerance), ![Paralysis](set:paralysis-tolerance), ![Nightmare](set:nightmare-tolerance), ![Mindfog](set:mindfog-tolerance), ![Fatigue](set:fatigue-tolerance), ![Blight](set:blight-tolerance), ![Manaburn](set:manaburn-tolerance), ![Atrophy](set:atrophy-tolerance), ![Bloodrot](set:bloodrot-tolerance).

- **3 pieces:** +50% tolerance to that corruption, halving its duration.
- **5 pieces add:** **+20% HP, +20% ATK and +20% DEF**, the same on all twelve, on top of the tolerance.

That 5-piece bonus is the biggest all-round bonus of any set, so any Tolerance set you've built still pays off against bosses without its corruption. Which to build first: [Which Tolerance set to build first](/blog/boss-corruptions-and-tolerance-sets).
