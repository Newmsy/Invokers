**On every boss, your Invokers gain Ultimate mana twice as fast on stages 1 to 12. Stage 13 drops that to ×1.5, and from stage 14 it's the normal rate (×1).**

It's the same for every relic and crest boss, Sorgott, Pentiax and all seven Omnissi.

| Stages | Ultimate mana | 900-mana Ultimate | 1,100-mana Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–12 | ×2 | 24.5 s | 30 s |
| 13 | ×1.5 | 32.7 s | 40 s |
| 14–20 | ×1 | 49.1 s | 60 s |

Times are seconds of attacking to fill an Ultimate from empty, with no Mana Generation. Ultimate mana fills with time spent attacking, not per hit. 155 Ultimates cost 900 mana, 76 cost 1,100.

## What it means

- **Ultimates come half as often from stage 14.** In a 60-second fight, a 900-mana Ultimate goes off twice on stage 12 but once on stage 14.
- **Ultimate-heavy Invokers lose the most.** If most of an Invoker's damage, healing or shields comes from its Ultimate, expect it to fall off harder than one built on Skills 1–3 and its Combo.
- **Stage 14 lands on top of the HP walls.** Most bosses' biggest HP jump sits at stages 13 to 16, and your Tolerance bonus is already gone by stage 10.
- **Mana Generation can't fully close the gap.** It would take +100% to match stage 12, and the curve tops out at +94.3% at 800 rating. 100 rating gives +42.3%, which brings a 900-mana Ultimate down to 34.5 s.

## What to do

- On stage 14 and up, lean on Invokers whose damage comes from Skills 1–3 and Combo, and don't count on an Ultimate to save a run.
- If a team needs its Ultimates, put some Mana Generation on them. The first 50 points pay the most (+0.5% each). Sets: ![Mana](set:).
- Compare stage 10 against stage 16 in the [Simulator](/simulator) to see how much DPS your Invoker loses.
- Check the boss's page on [Bosses](/bosses) for its HP walls before spending energy there.
