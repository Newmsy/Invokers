**Every boss follows the same curve for level, enemy ACC and RES, and your Tolerance bonus. Only HP, ATK and DEF differ.**

- **Stage 10 is the turning point.** Your Tolerance bonus drops from +25% to 0, and enemy RES jumps from 115 to 174 (then 275 at stage 11).
- **From stage 11 it's a penalty**, reaching −100% at stages 19 and 20.
- **Stage 1 to 20, boss HP grows about ×180 to ×800.** The steepest late steps are usually at stage 15 or 16.
- Relic bosses, the clan boss and Pentiax cost 20 energy a run from stage 17. Crest bosses and Omnissi top out at 14.

Every boss page now has these numbers above its stage table, with the biggest wall highlighted. Start at [Bosses](/bosses).

## The shared curve

| Stage | Boss level | Enemy ACC | Enemy RES | Your Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 0 | 50 | +75% |
| 5 | 40 | 15 | 60 | +45% |
| 9 | 60 | 30 | 115 | +25% |
| 10 | 65 | 40 | 174 | 0% |
| 11 | 70 | 50 | 275 | −10% |
| 15 | 90 | 90 | 440 | −50% |
| 18 | 115 | 140 | 555 | −80% |
| 20 | 150 | 175 | 635 | −100% |

## What to do with it

- Stages 1–9 give your Invokers a Tolerance bonus. From stage 10, bring the right Tolerance set: *Which Tolerance set to build first*.
- Expect a wall around stage 15. Check the boss's page for its exact jump before spending energy there.
- Plan energy for the stages you clear on [Events](/events).
