**Weapon, Shield and Helmet always roll flat ATK, DEF and HP. The other five relics are where you pick a stat.**

- Upgrades go to **level 20**. Every 5 levels adds a substat or upgrades one.
- Rarity sets the starting substats: Common 0, Uncommon 1, Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4.
- Accessories cost exactly the same to max as relics of the same stars and rarity.

## Relic main stats

| Slot | Main stat |
|---|---|
| Weapon | ATK |
| Shield | DEF |
| Helmet | HP |
| Pauldrons | ATK%, DEF% or HP% |
| Gauntlets | ATK%, DEF%, HP%, **Crit Rate, Crit DMG**, Combo SPD, Skill SPD, Skill RECOV or Mana Gen |
| Chestplate | ATK%, DEF%, HP%, **ACC, RES**, Combo SPD, Skill SPD, Skill RECOV or Mana Gen |
| Belt | ATK%, DEF%, HP%, Combo SPD, Skill SPD, Skill RECOV, Mana Gen or **Instinct** |
| Boots | ATK%, DEF%, HP%, Combo SPD, Skill SPD, Skill RECOV, Mana Gen or **Move SPD** |

Gauntlets are the only Crit main stat and Chestplates the only ACC or RES one. Plan around those two.

## Substats

- Each new substat rolls 70%, 80%, 90% or 100% of its base value, all equally likely.
- An upgrade adds another roll to one substat. Legendary pieces get 4 upgrades, Epic 3, Rare 2, Uncommon 1.
- Grades run D to S by total roll. A Legendary substat that takes all 4 upgrades ends between 350% and 430%, grade B to S.

## Sets

3 pieces give the first bonus, 5 pieces add a second. You can mix a 3-piece and a 5-piece of different sets. Boss relics are 3★ or better and can be Legendary; campaign relics are Common or Uncommon, up to 5★. Every set: [Every gear set](/blog/every-gear-set) and [Gear sets](/gear).

## Accessories

Accessories come from the clan boss [Sorgott](/bosses/sorgott), two per kill. They have no sets, only fit Invokers of the faction printed on them, and you can hold 1,000.

| Slot | Opens at | Main stat |
|---|---|---|
| Amulet | Start | ATK or DEF |
| Earrings | Awakening 2★ | HP |
| Banner | Awakening 4★ | ATK%, DEF%, HP%, Crit Rate or Crit DMG |
| Talisman | Awakening 5★ | Combo SPD, Skill SPD, Skill RECOV or Mana Gen |
| Flask | Awakening 6★ | ACC, RES or Instinct |
