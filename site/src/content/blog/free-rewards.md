**Do your Daily Quests and reach the last Playtime milestone every day. Together that's about 2,400 Gems a month.**

- Daily things reset at **00:00 UTC**; the Arena resets Monday 11:00 UTC.
- Spend free tokens before they cap: Arena stores only 10.
- Keep ![Nirvelle the Soulraiser](hero:) from the day-90 login: she's A or S in 10 of our 19 [tier lists](/tiers).

## Every day

| Source | What you get |
|---|---|
| Playtime (5, 15, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 200 minutes) | Gold, Memorium, Energy, a Rare Codex, Arena and Multi-Battle Tokens, Gems (about 1,200 a month) |
| Daily Quests | 35,000 Gold, 30 Gems, 50 Energy and Star and Orb fragments each day (about 1,200 Gems a month) |
| Multi-Battle Tokens | 10 at reset, 5 from Playtime, 5 from Daily Quests: **20 a day** |
| Arena Tokens | 1 an hour plus 5 from Playtime: **29 a day** |

What the tokens would cost: 20 Multi-Battle Tokens are worth 50 Gems (100 Gems buys 40), and 29 Arena Tokens 145 Gems (50 Gems buys 10).

## Every week and month

- **Weekly:** finish your Daily Quests 7 times for 25 Radiant Star fragments, about one free Radiant Star a month. Weekly quests add XP and Gold Boosters, XP potions and Memorium.
- **Monthly:** Titanhearts, a Divine Star for finishing them all, 500 Energy, 3★ and 4★ Echoes and 500,000 Gold.
- **Arena:** a weekly chest by league if you play 10 offence battles ([Arena guide](/blog/arena-guide)).

## One-off rewards

- **Campaign stars:** up to 5 per stage, 50 per chapter, on Normal, Hard and Insane. Milestones give Stars, Orbs, Codexes and Echoes.
- **[Progress Quests](/rewards/progress-quests):** 634 quests over 41 pages, with a completion reward per page and 5,810 Gems in all.
- **[Achievements](/rewards/achievements):** first clear of every boss stage, then 10 and 20 clears, for Codexes and Prisms.
