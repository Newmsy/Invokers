**A chroma is an alternate colour version of an Invoker or Titan. The higher the rarity, the better the chroma chance: 2% for a Legendary Invoker, 0.033% for a Common.**

- ![Divine Star](item:) is the best chroma item by far: about 1 pull in 94 gives a chroma of some kind, and 1 in 750 gives a chroma Legendary.
- ![Destiny Star](item:) gives a chroma Legendary about 1 pull in 9,400.
- ![Miracle Orb](item:) gives a chroma Mythic Titan about 1 pull in 5,000.

These are odds at the published rates, before mercy. They don't tell you what your next pull will be. Run your own numbers in the [Summons odds calculator](/summons).

## Chroma chance by rarity

Once you've pulled an Invoker or Titan of that rarity, this is the chance it's a chroma.

| Rarity | Invokers | Titans |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 0.033% | – |
| Uncommon | 0.05% | – |
| Rare | 0.2% | 0.05% |
| Epic | 1% | 0.25% |
| Legendary | 2% | 0.5% |
| Mythic | – | 2% |

Mercy makes Legendaries come a little more often over a long run, so the Legendary rows are slightly better in practice. See *What 130 Destiny Stars really buy you* for how mercy works (estimated).

## The takeaway

- Chasing a chroma Legendary Invoker? Divine Stars are about **12 times better** per pull than Destiny Stars.
- Most chromas you'll see are Rares and Epics, simply because those come up most.
- Titans are four times less likely to be chroma than Invokers of the same rarity, except Mythics (2%).
