Of the 40 loading-screen tips, the checkable ones mostly hold up. One gets the maths wrong, and a few big things are missing.

## Confirmed

| Tip | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "Ascending your Invokers increases their level cap." | True: each star adds 10 levels to the cap, up to level 60 at 6★. ([Levelling vs Awakening](/blog/levelling-vs-awakening)) |
| "Upgrading Skills Unlocks New Strength." | Every hero damage skill hits exactly twice as hard at level 11 as at level 1. ([Maxing a skill doubles its damage](/blog/maxing-a-skill)) |
| "A Relic's Rarity decides how many Substats it has at Level 0." | Common 0, Uncommon 1, Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4. +5, +10, +15 and +20 add a substat until there are 4, then upgrade one, so a Legendary spends all four on upgrades and a Common on unlocks. |
| "Equip 3 or 5 Relics from the same Set." | None of the [31 sets](/gear) has a bonus beyond 5 pieces. |
| "Each Boss has two different Corruptions." | True for all 16 bosses. ([Which Tolerance set to build first](/blog/boss-corruptions-and-tolerance-sets)) |
| "Common, Uncommon, and Rare Invokers each have one Tolerance. Epic and Legendary Invokers have two." | Matches skill upgrades: only Epics and Legendaries have a second Tolerance to level. |
| "You get 30 free Spire Keys every day, 5 per Element." | Six elements × 5 = 30: ![](item:fire-spire-key)![](item:water-spire-key)![](item:earth-spire-key)![](item:wind-spire-key)![](item:light-spire-key)![](item:dark-spire-key) |
| "You need more Accuracy than the enemy's Resistance to land debuffs consistently." | Resist is driven by Resistance minus Accuracy. The first 100 points of difference count for far more than the next thousand. |

## True, but the maths is off

![](item:1-day-xp-boost) **"50% More XP means 50% faster levelling."** You need two thirds as many battles: a third less time, not half. Still worth activating.

## What the tips leave out

- **Gear gold:** about 80% of the gold to max a 6★ piece goes on +15 to +20. ([Where your gold goes on gear](/blog/where-your-gear-gold-goes))
- **Stats that stop:** Instinct does nothing past 362, and Skill Speed's first 100 points beat the 1,000 from 1,000 to 2,000. ([Stats that stop working](/blog/stats-that-stop-working))
- **Mercy:** no tip mentions it, but summons have it. ([What 130 Destiny Stars really buy you](/blog/destiny-star-odds))

## Tip numbers not checked yet

Elemental advantage (+30% damage dealt, 30% less taken), Titan Invokation lasting 12 seconds, and AoE target limits by rarity (7 for Common and Uncommon, 8 Rare, 9 Epic, 10 Legendary).

*Numbers for game version 0.61.1516.*
