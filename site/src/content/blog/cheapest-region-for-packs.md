**It isn't worth it. Most regions charge within about 2% of the US price once tax is taken out.** The cheapest all-in prices are Indonesia (about 7% under the US) and Pakistan (about 5% under), and Indonesia's discount disappears on its bigger pack. Canada's CA$39.99 looks cheap (about **$28.11**), but Canadian sales tax of 5-15% is added at checkout, so you pay $29.52-$32.33.

**Changing region with a VPN may break the game's terms of service and put your account at risk.** A saving of a few percent isn't worth losing an account over.

- **Prices are the US price plus local tax.** The UK and Eurozone pay most only because their VAT is highest.
- **Indonesia and Pakistan are the real discounts:** about 7% and 5% under the US with no tax shown. Hong Kong has no sales tax, so its price is simply the US price, rounded down.
- **Mexico is the odd one out:** +8% with no tax shown. Its 16% VAT is probably included without being itemised.
- **India is cheap before tax but not after:** if its 18% GST is included, the pack is about 11% under the US before tax, but you still pay 5% more at checkout.

## Before you try it

- **A cheap small pack doesn't mean a cheap big one.** Indonesia's $99.99 pack is IDR 1,790,000, about $100, so the 7% saving is gone.
- **Exchange rates move,** so the order can change week to week.
- **Card fees for foreign currency (usually 1-3%)** cancel out most of the gap.
- **Where the price comes from:** Web and Desktop prices follow your current location. App Store and Google Play prices follow your card's tax residency, so a VPN doesn't change them.

The developers announced that Web Shop and Desktop prices are now in local currency with VAT included, matching the App Store and Google Play or a little cheaper. Both also give **+20% VIP Points** over mobile: 120 points per $1 instead of 100. That beats any region swap, with no risk.
