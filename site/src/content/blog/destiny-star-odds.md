A Destiny Star gives a Legendary **0.53%** of the time. Without mercy, half of all players need **130 pulls** or more for their first one. Mercy (pity) rules change that a lot.

- Our best estimate: a guaranteed first Legendary by pull 50, then one every 190 pulls at worst.
- Radiant Stars are the better way to chase one specific Light or Dark Legendary: their pool is half the size.
- Divine Stars are a different world: a Legendary in 10 pulls is a coin flip.

Every hero in a rarity has the same weight. The Destiny Legendary pool has 38 heroes (every Fire, Water, Earth and Wind Legendary except Ardell The Torrent, who can't be summoned), so a **specific** Legendary is about **1 in 7,170 pulls**. The Radiant pool has 19, so about 1 in 3,585.

## The mercy counters

> **Estimated:** the game doesn't explain its mercy rules. This is our best estimate and hasn't been confirmed in game.

| Item | Rarity | Mercy count | Most likely meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Destiny Star | Legendary | 50 first, then 190 | First Legendary by pull 50, then one by every 190th pull without one |
| Radiant Star | Legendary | 200 | One by every 200th pull without one |
| Divine Star | Legendary | 15 | One by every 15th pull without one |
| Wonder Orb | Legendary | 190 | As Destiny, without the first-50 rule |
| Miracle Orb | Legendary | 15 | As Divine (Mythic has no mercy) |
| Fate, Destiny, Radiant | Epic | 15, limit 3, then 30 | An Epic by pull 15 the first 3 times, then by pull 30 |

Still unknown: whether a natural Legendary resets the counter, and whether items share one.
