## What happened

- **11 Invokers and 3 Titans**, including the first Mythical Titan.
- **Player-voted Invoker ratings:** each Invoker is rated 1–5 per boss and mode. S is 4.8 and up, A 4.5–4.79, B 4.0–4.49, C 3.5–3.99 and D 3.0–3.49.
- **Quality of life:** battle info in the pause menu, in-game alerts, smarter healing and reviving, Titan lock, and a cap on Gold League point losses.
