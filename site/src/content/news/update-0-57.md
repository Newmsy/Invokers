**Update 0.57 added player-voted Invoker ratings on 30 April 2026.** Each Invoker is rated 1–5 per boss and mode: S is 4.8 and up, A 4.5–4.79, B 4.0–4.49, C 3.5–3.99 and D 3.0–3.49. Our own [Tier lists](/tiers) rank every Invoker and Titan for each boss and mode, with what each pick brings.

It also brought 11 Invokers and 3 Titans, including the first Mythical Titan; browse them all on [Titans](/titans). Quality of life improved with battle info in the pause menu, in-game alerts, smarter healing and reviving, a Titan lock, and a cap on Gold League point losses.
