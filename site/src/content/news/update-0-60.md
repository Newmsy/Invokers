**Update 0.60 added Chroma Invokers and Titans on 12 August 2026**, rare alternate colours. The odds run from 2% for a Legendary Invoker or Mythic Titan down to 0.033% for a Common, and ![Divine Star](item:) is the best chroma item. See *Chroma odds, item by item* on the [Blog](/blog).

**The VIP Pass** has 25 levels and costs 300, 1,400 or 3,000 ![Gems](item:) for 1, 7 or 30 days. Per day, the 30-day pass is a third of the price of the 1-day pass:

| Pass | Gems | Gems per day |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day | 300 | 300 |
| 7 days | 1,400 | 200 |
| 30 days | 3,000 | 100 |

There's a new home screen too. Chases get their own leaderboards from September, and Pentiax Spark Hunt and Campaign Rush join them. Taranis's Pulse Singularity was fixed, and 9 new Invokers and 2 Mythical Titans arrived.
