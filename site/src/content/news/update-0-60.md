## What happened

- **Chroma Invokers and Titans:** rare alternate colours.
- **VIP Pass:** 25 levels. Costs 300, 1,400 or 3,000 ![Gems](item:) for 1, 7 or 30 days.
- **New home screen.** Chases get their own leaderboards from September, and Pentiax Spark Hunt and Campaign Rush join them.
- **Fix:** Taranis's Pulse Singularity. 9 new Invokers and 2 Mythical Titans.

## Our take

- **Chroma odds:** 2% for a Legendary Invoker and 2% for a Mythic Titan, down to 0.033% for a Common. ![Divine Star](item:) is the best chroma item. See *Chroma odds, item by item* on the [Blog](/blog).
- **VIP Pass per day:**

| Pass | Gems | Gems per day |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day | 300 | 300 |
| 7 days | 1,400 | 200 |
| 30 days | 3,000 | 100 |

The 30-day pass is a third of the price per day of the 1-day pass.
