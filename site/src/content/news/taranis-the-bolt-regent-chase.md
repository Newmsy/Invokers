## What happened

Update 0.59 brought Events and Tournaments, and with them the first chase: the **Taranis Chase**. It started the week after the 7 July update and ran for 3 weeks. Players earned ![Crown](item:) Crowns from the tournaments inside it, with double points during the chase, and spent them in the Event Shop on ![Taranis the Bolt Regent](hero:). Update 0.60 later fixed Taranis's Ultimate, Pulse Singularity.

## Our take

- **Shock on a timer.** The Ultimate spawns a Shocksphere for 10s that hits every second. Shocked enemies can't move or use Skills and take 125% damage.
- **Two big debuffs at once.** Skill 3 hits up to 10 enemies with **100% ACC Down** and **40% ATK Down** for 16s.
- **Debuffs fuel the Ultimate.** Skill 2 gives 25 Ultimate Mana per debuff on the target (up to 500), and its crits cut Skill 3's cooldown by 5s.
- **Sturdy for a Ranged.** Top HP and DEF among the 8 Legendary Ranged.
- **Where Taranis shines:** S in Arena and against Koltmos, A on most other lists.
- **Missed the chase?** Taranis is also in the summon pool. See [Summons](/summons) for the odds.
