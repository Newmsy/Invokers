**Update 0.59 brought Events and Tournaments, and with them the first chase: the Taranis Chase.** It ran for 3 weeks, from Monday 13 July to Sunday 2 August 2026. Players earned ![](item:Crown) Crowns from the tournaments inside it, with double points during the chase, and spent them in the Event Shop on ![Taranis the Bolt Regent](hero:). Taranis joined the Star summons the day it started, so if you missed the chase, [Summons](/summons) has the odds.

The first chase had teething problems. A 22 July fix made 10x summons score full points in the Summon Surge, with 20 Crowns and a Codex of each rarity as an apology, and Crowns missing at the end were restored on 3 August. Update 0.60 later fixed Taranis's Ultimate, Pulse Singularity.

The kit shocks from range and feeds on debuffs:

- **Shock on a timer.** The Ultimate spawns a Shocksphere for 10s that hits every second. Shocked enemies can't move or use Skills and take 125% damage.
- **Two big debuffs at once.** Skill 3 hits up to 10 enemies with **100% ACC Down** and **40% ATK Down** for 16s.
- **Debuffs fuel the Ultimate.** Skill 2 gives 25 Ultimate Mana per debuff on the target (up to 500), and its crits cut Skill 3's cooldown by 5s.
- **Sturdy for a Ranged.** Top HP and DEF among the 8 Legendary Ranged.

That makes Taranis an S pick in the Arena and against Koltmos, and an A on most other lists.
