**Update 0.58 brought Background Battles (from level 25) and in-game Chat on 11 June 2026**, plus new languages.

**Pentiax was reworked:** Roar of the Eternal replaces Fall of the Void. See the [Pentiax guide](/bosses/pentiax) and the [Pentiax tier list](/tiers/invokers/pentiax) for the fight today.

**Epic guarantees changed.** ![](item:Radiant%20Star) Radiant Stars now guarantee an Epic every 30 Stars, and Destiny and Fate Stars count towards one shared 30-Star Epic guarantee. Every Star's odds are on [Summons](/summons).

**Login rewards now run to day 270**, with three free Invokers along the way: ![Nightguard Aleksu](hero:) at day 210, ![Almea](hero:) at 240 and ![Elder King Thalbrennar](hero:) at 270. That's two Epics and a Legendary just for logging in; their numbers are below.

6 Invokers and 6 Titans arrived too, including the first Legendary Cambion Titan.
