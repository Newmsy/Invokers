**Update 0.59 brought Events and Tournaments on 7 July 2026.** Most Tournaments run for 3 days, with personal, local, global and challenge rewards, collected through a new Inbox. Events cost Energy: the [Events planner](/events) works out how much a whole chase takes from the highest stage you clear on each boss, and how many Gems would cover a shortfall.

**The first chase came with it:** the 3-week Taranis Chase, with Crowns for an Event Shop and double points during the chase. The [Taranis spotlight](/news/taranis-the-bolt-regent-chase) covers what the kit does; Taranis is also in the summon pool.

Also new: the Gear Swap Pass, timed Campaign Stars, and 8 Invokers and 3 Titans.
