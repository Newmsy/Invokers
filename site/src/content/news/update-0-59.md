## What happened

- **Events and Tournaments:** most run for 3 days, with personal, local, global and challenge rewards, plus a new Inbox.
- **The first chase:** the 3-week Taranis Chase, with Crowns for an Event Shop and double points during the chase.
- **Also new:** the Gear Swap Pass, timed Campaign Stars, 8 Invokers and 3 Titans, including Taranis, who is also in the summon pool.

## Our take

- **Events cost Energy.** The [Events planner](/events) works out how much Energy a whole chase takes from the highest stage you clear on each boss, and how many Gems would cover a shortfall.
- **Taranis** was the first chase prize. See the [Taranis spotlight](/news/taranis-the-bolt-regent-chase) for what the kit does.
