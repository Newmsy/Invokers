**The Ardell Chase, the second chase, ran for 3 weeks from Monday 10 August 2026.** Players earned ![](item:Crown) Crowns in tournaments and spent them in the Event Shop on ![Ardell The Torrent](hero:), a new Legendary Eldrin Melee Attacker. Ardell showed up on the Event Calendar first and was announced on the day the chase began, when Ardell also joined the Star summons.

Where Taranis shocks from range, Ardell slams into the front line, and the kit is built around debuffs:

- **Debuff engine.** Skill 2 puts **50% DEF Down** on up to 10 enemies. Skill 1 moves a debuff onto a new target. The Ultimate adds **Mindworm** (no Passives for 18s), then extends up to 4 debuffs by 10s.
- **Buff thief.** Skill 3 hits for 640% ATK and steals 4 buffs.
- **Hits hard.** 13th-highest ATK of all 58 Legendaries when fully awakened.

That makes Ardell an S pick on Sakiel, Yskara and two Omnissi lists.
