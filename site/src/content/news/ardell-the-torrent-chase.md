## What happened

The **Ardell Chase**, the second chase, ran for 3 weeks from Monday 10 August 2026. Players earned ![Crown](item:) Crowns in tournaments and spent them in the Event Shop on ![Ardell The Torrent](hero:), a new Legendary Eldrin Melee Attacker. Ardell appeared on the Event Calendar first and was announced on the day the chase began, when Ardell also joined the Star summons.

## Our take

- **Up close, unlike Taranis.** Taranis shocks from range; Ardell slams into the front line.
- **Debuff engine.** Skill 2 puts **50% DEF Down** on up to 10 enemies. Skill 1 moves a debuff onto a new target. The Ultimate adds **Mindworm** (no Passives for 18s), then extends up to 4 debuffs by 10s.
- **Buff thief.** Skill 3 hits for 640% ATK and steals 4 buffs.
- **Hits hard.** 13th-highest ATK of all 58 Legendaries when fully awakened.
- **Where Ardell shines:** S on Sakiel, Yskara and two Omnissi lists.
