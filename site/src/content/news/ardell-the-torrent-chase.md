## What happened

![Ardell The Torrent](hero:) was the top prize of a 21-day **Get Legendary chase**. Players earned chase points from the events running inside it and claimed Ardell at the top tier.

## Our take

- **Event-only.** Ardell isn't in any summon pool, so the chase was the way to get them.
- **Debuff engine.** Skill 2 puts **50% DEF Down** on up to 10 enemies, Skill 1 moves a debuff onto a new target, and the Ultimate adds **Mindworm** (no Passives for 18s) then extends up to 4 debuffs by 10s.
- **Buff thief.** Skill 3 hits for 640% ATK and steals 4 buffs.
- **Hits hard.** 13th-highest ATK of all Legendaries when fully awakened, with S placings on Sakiel, Yskara and two Omnissi lists.
