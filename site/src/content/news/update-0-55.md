**The Arena opened with Update 0.55 on 1 April 2026.** You set a defence team, then attack other players' defences to climb the leagues. Leagues, tokens, cooldowns and rewards are in the [Arena guide](/blog/arena-guide), and who to bring is on the [Arena tier list](/tiers/invokers/arena).

Arena battles changed stats from day one: Invokers had 8x HP and Skills started at 70% cooldown, while Titans had 95% lower stats and a 90-second cooldown. In May, Skills changed to start at **50%** cooldown and Titan Mana dropped from 5x to **4x**; see [the May Arena hotfix](/news/arena-crowd-control-hotfix).

**The Trophy Room** turns Arena Trophies into stat boosts for every Invoker of an element. Those boosts are why an Invoker's stats in the game are higher than on a hero page. Costs are on [Trophies](/trophies) and in *Trophy Room cost* on the [Blog](/blog).

Titans were rebalanced to hit bosses harder, with boss HP adjusted to match. Player profiles and avatars arrived, along with more than one profile and play across devices. You can also unlock Garudas with Gems and choose your Titans' Invokation order, and there were 11 new Invokers (2 Legendaries) and 2 Titans.
