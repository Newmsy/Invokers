## What happened

- **The Arena:** set a defence team, then attack other players' defences to climb the leagues.
- **Trophy Room:** spend Arena Trophies on stat boosts for every Invoker of an element.
- **Player profiles and avatars**, more than one profile, and play across devices.
- **Titan rebalance and a boss stats overhaul:** Titans hit bosses harder, and boss HP was adjusted to match.
- **Also new:** unlock Garudas with Gems, choose your Titans' Invokation order, 11 new Invokers (2 Legendaries) and 2 Titans.

**How the Arena changed stats at launch:** Invokers had 8x HP and Skills started at 70% cooldown. Titans had 95% lower stats and a 90-second cooldown. In May, Skills changed to start at **50%** cooldown and Titan Mana dropped from 5x to **4x**. See [the May Arena hotfix](/news/arena-crowd-control-hotfix).

- **Arena rules today:** leagues, tokens, cooldowns and rewards are in the [Arena guide](/blog/arena-guide), and who to bring is on the [Arena tier list](/tiers/invokers/arena).
