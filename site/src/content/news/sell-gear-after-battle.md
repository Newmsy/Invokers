**You can now sell or crush gear right where it drops.** Tap a piece on the post-battle or multi-battle screen and the popup offers **Equip**, **Crush** and **Sell**, so spare pieces no longer have to wait for a trip to the inventory.

The returns are the same as from the inventory. A 5★ Rare belt from Ulgorim stage 16 crushes for 125 ![](item:Memorium) Memorium or sells for 31,000 ![](item:Gold) Gold. Crushing pays by stars only, whatever the rarity:

| Stars | Crush for Memorium | Sell Rare | Sell Epic | Sell Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5★ | 125 | 31,000 | 47,000 | 71,000 |
| 6★ | 250 | 61,000 | 92,000 | 138,000 |

Memorium only goes into gear upgrades, so crush when you're short of it and sell when Gold is the bottleneck. Every star and rarity is in [Gear upgrades and Memorium](/gear-upgrades/#selling).
