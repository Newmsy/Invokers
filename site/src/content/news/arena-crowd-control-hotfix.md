**On 14 May 2026 a hotfix went after the Shock and Stun meta in the Arena.** Skills now start at **50% cooldown** (was 70%) and Titan Mana Generation dropped to **4x** (was 5x). Both still hold: your time to first cast is 0.5 × base cooldown ÷ (1 + Skill Recovery %), as the [Arena guide](/blog/arena-guide) explains. Matchmaking ranges changed in every league, and the Gold League loss limit on defence was removed.

The control Titans were cut too: ![Amprix](titan:)'s Invokation Shock and ![Cairnai](titan:)'s Invokation Stun now last **2s** (were 4s).

### The crowd control caps

The biggest change capped how long any Invoker's control could last, by rarity and skill type:

| Invokers | Single-target Skill | AoE Skill | Single-target Ultimate | AoE Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rare and below | 2s | 1.5s | 3s | 2s |
| Fire, Water, Earth, Wind Epics | 3s | 2s | 4s | 2.5s |
| Light and Dark Epics | 3.5s | 2.5s | 4s | 2.5s |
| Fire, Water, Earth, Wind Legendaries | 4s | 3s | 5s | 3s |
| Light and Dark Legendaries | 5s | 3.5s | 6s | 4s |

**It lasted a day.** On 15 May almost every Invoker went back to how they were. The Titan changes stayed, Virtus the Heavensent lost a little more damage, and Shredripper Matescu's Ultimate became a 3s AoE Shock (5s at max level). Arena-only duration modifiers and diminishing returns arrived instead in [Update 0.58](/news/update-0-58).

### The rest of the hotfix

17 Invokers now place Decrease Combo SPD *before* their hit, which helps against Dark Omnissi. The Paragon set gives Move SPD in battle too, and Virtus lost 3–5% on every Skill.
