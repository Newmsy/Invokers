## What happened

**14 May hotfix, aimed at the Shock and Stun meta in the Arena:**

- **Arena:** Skills start at **50% cooldown** (was 70%). Titan Mana Generation **4x** (was 5x). Matchmaking ranges changed in every league. The Gold League loss limit on defence was removed.
- **Titans:** ![Amprix](titan:)'s Invokation Shock and ![Cairnai](titan:)'s Invokation Stun last **2s** (were 4s).
- **Caps on crowd control from every Invoker**, by rarity and skill type:

| Invokers | Single-target Skill | AoE Skill | Single-target Ultimate | AoE Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rare and below | 2s | 1.5s | 3s | 2s |
| Fire, Water, Earth, Wind Epics | 3s | 2s | 4s | 2.5s |
| Light and Dark Epics | 3.5s | 2.5s | 4s | 2.5s |
| Fire, Water, Earth, Wind Legendaries | 4s | 3s | 5s | 3s |
| Light and Dark Legendaries | 5s | 3.5s | 6s | 4s |

- **Also:** 17 Invokers place Decrease Combo SPD *before* their hit (good against Dark Omnissi). ![Ballus the Bull](hero:) and ![Atanna the Convincer](hero:) deal DoT from DEF instead of ATK. The Paragon set gives Move SPD in battle too. Virtus the Heavensent lost 3–5% on every Skill.

**15 May: the caps were reverted.** Almost every Invoker went back to how they were. The Titan changes stayed, Virtus lost a little more, and Shredripper Matescu's Ultimate became a 3s AoE Shock (5s at max level). Arena-only duration modifiers and diminishing returns came instead in [Update 0.58](/news/update-0-58).

## Our take

- **The 50% start still holds.** Time to first cast = 0.5 × base cooldown ÷ (1 + Skill Recovery %). See the [Arena guide](/blog/arena-guide).
- **Tank DoTs:** Ballus and Atanna get more from DEF than ATK, so build them like Tanks. Their numbers are below.
