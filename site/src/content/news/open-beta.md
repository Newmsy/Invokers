**Invokers entered Open Beta on 23 July 2026**, on iOS and Android in almost every country. It isn't the global launch: content and tuning carry on in beta, and the launch will be announced separately. The game wasn't yet available in South Korea, Japan, Hong Kong, China, Taiwan, Russia, Belarus, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Myanmar or Cambodia.

The day before, a fix made 10x summons score full points in the Invoker Summon Surge, and everyone at level 10 or higher got a Rare, Epic and Legendary Codex and 20 Crowns.
