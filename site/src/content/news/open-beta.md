## What happened

- **Open Beta from 23 July 2026:** the game is on iOS and Android in almost every country.
- **Not yet available in:** South Korea, Japan, Hong Kong, China, Taiwan, Russia, Belarus, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Myanmar and Cambodia.
- **Not the global launch.** Content and tuning continue in beta, and the launch will be announced separately.
- **The day before**, a fix made 10x summons score full points in the Invoker Summon Surge. Everyone at level 10 or higher got a Rare, Epic and Legendary Codex and 20 Crowns.
