**The Grimnall Chase runs for 3 weeks, from Monday 12 October to Monday 2 November 2026 (12:00 UTC).** Tournaments inside it pay ![](item:Pumpkin) Pumpkins at point milestones, and Pumpkins buy [Grimnall the Hollowed](/news/grimnall-the-hollowed-spotlight/) and other rewards in the Event Shop. Sign up by Thursday 15 October; it's open to levels 1–100.

### Where the Pumpkins come from

**Grimnall costs 170 of the 200 Pumpkins on offer**, so plan to clear nearly every milestone:

| Tournament | Milestones (points) | Pumpkins |
|---|---|---|
| Ulgorim, Althar, Sakiel Relic Hunts | 102,000 each | 10 each |
| Gear Up (×3) | 24k / 48k / 72k | 10 at each (30) |
| Arena Rush (×3) | 36k / 72k / 108k | 10 at each (30) |
| Invoker Summon Surge (×2) | 200k / 400k | 10 at each (20) |
| Titan Summon Surge | 180,000 | 20 |
| Invoker Power Up | 180,000 | 10 |
| Sorgott Accessory Hunt | 120,000 | 10 |
| Titan Power Up | 60,000 | 10 |
| Campaign Rush | 48,000 | 10 |
| Crest Hunt | 36,000 | 10 |
| Omnissi Essence Hunt | 32,000 | 10 |
| Pentiax Spark Hunt | 32,000 | 10 |
| **Total** | | **200** |

Compared with the Senhachi Chase, Crest Hunt now pays once instead of twice, the Sorgott Accessory Hunt joins with 10, and Campaign Rush needs 48,000 points instead of 4,800.

### The Event Shop

Prices in Pumpkins, with the limit: Grimnall 170 (1) · ![Divine Star](item:) 50 (1) · ![Miracle Orb](item:) 50 (1) · ![Legendary Codex](item:) 20 (2) · ![Legendary Prism](item:) 20 (2) · Epic Codex and Epic Prism 10 (4) · ![Destiny Star](item:) and Wonder Orb 10 (10) · Rare Codex and Rare Prism 5 (10) · Large XP Potion 1 (10).

**The last 30 Pumpkins go furthest on a Legendary Codex plus an Epic Codex or Prism.** If you miss a milestone or two, drop the Epic item first. Pumpkins left when the chase ends are spent for you and the items go to your Inbox.

To fit the chase around other events, see the [Events planner](/events/).
