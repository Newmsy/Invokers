**The second Sorgott Accessory Hunt runs from 5 to 12 October 2026 (12:00 UTC), and every point target has roughly doubled.** The first Hunt (28 September to 5 October) was an easy introduction. Now that players have teams for [Sorgott](/bosses/sorgott) and a few Accessories, the targets match other Tournaments: the full personal track takes **100,000 points**, up from 51,000.

The rewards are the same, with three extra ![](item:Energy) Energy drops added, so the track now pays **700 Energy** instead of 200:

| Reward | Points now | First Hunt |
|---|---|---|
| 200,000 ![](item:Gold) Gold | 1,000 | 500 |
| 100 Energy | 2,000 | new |
| 3 Refined Astral Essence | 3,000 | 1,500 |
| 50 ![](item:Gems) Gems | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| 3 Pure Astral Essence | 10,000 | 5,000 |
| 150 Energy | 13,000 | new |
| 40 Multi-Battle Tokens | 18,000 | 9,000 |
| ![Epic Codex](item:) | 24,000 | 12,000 |
| 200 Energy | 32,000 | 16,000 |
| ![Rare Prism](item:) | 60,000 | 30,000 |
| Epic Codex | 70,000 | 35,000 |
| 250 Energy | 90,000 | new |
| ![Legendary Codex](item:) | 100,000 | 51,000 |

Points come from Accessory drops, and 6★ Accessories are worth far more than the rest: in the first Hunt a 6★ Legendary scored 2,500 points and a 5★ Legendary 450. At those values, 100,000 points is 40 6★ Legendary drops or over 220 5★ Legendary ones, so the highest Sorgott stage you can clear reliably is where the points are. Who to bring is on the [Sorgott tier list](/tiers/invokers/sorgott), and the [Event calendar](/event-calendar) has the full track and the rest of this week's Tournaments.
