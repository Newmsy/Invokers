**The second Sorgott Accessory Hunt runs from 5 to 12 October 2026 (12:00 UTC), and its point targets are going up.** The first Hunt (28 September to 5 October) was an easy introduction: its personal track topped out at 51,000 points for a ![Legendary Codex](item:). Now that players have teams for [Sorgott](/bosses/sorgott) and a few Accessories, the targets move up to match other Tournaments. The new numbers weren't announced.

**The personal track also gets more ![](item:Energy) Energy**, to fund the extra Sorgott runs. The first Hunt paid 200 Energy at 16,000 points.

Points come from Accessory drops, and 6★ Accessories are worth far more than the rest: 800 to 2,500 points each in the first Hunt, against 230 to 450 for a 5★. So the highest Sorgott stage you can clear reliably is where the points are. Who to bring is on the [Sorgott tier list](/tiers/invokers/sorgott), and the [Event calendar](/event-calendar) has the rest of this week's Tournaments.
