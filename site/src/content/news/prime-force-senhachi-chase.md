## What happened

![Prime Force Senhachi](hero:) was the top prize of a 21-day **Get Legendary chase**. Players earned chase points from the events running inside it and claimed Senhachi at the top tier.

## Our take

- **Glass cannon.** 3rd-highest ATK of all 58 Legendaries when fully awakened, but 52nd for HP and DEF.
- **Crowd control everywhere.** Skill 3 launches up to 10 enemies for 4s with **50% ATK Down**; the Ultimate launches 4 times and adds **10s to every Skill cooldown** on each target it hits.
- **Single-target setup.** Skill 2 lands **45% DEF Down** and **25% Skill Recovery Down** for 15s.
- **Titan Mana.** Each Ultimate gives 150 Titan Mana, so your Titan comes in sooner.
- **Where they shine.** S on Pentiax, Koltmos and Omnissi (Wind).
