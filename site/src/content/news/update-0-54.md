**Update 0.54 landed on 9 March 2026 with Spires Auto-Climb and a big Bleed and Burn nerf.** Auto-Climb spends your Keys and climbs for free; what each floor pays is on [Spires](/spires). Quests and logins grew too: Bonus Quests, more Progress Quests, Weekly and Monthly Quests that adapt to you, and longer Daily Login Rewards.

### A Legendary on your 50th Destiny Star

New players now get a one-time Legendary on their 50th ![Destiny Star](item:). Existing players weren't left out: from 13 March, anyone who had summoned at least 50 Destiny Stars before 0.54 could claim a free Legendary from the ![](item:Divine%20Star) Divine Star summon screen, and players under 190 had their counter moved on by 50. That makes **two Legendaries guaranteed in 240 Destiny Stars**, one at the 50th and one at the 190th. Rates and mercy counts are on [Summons](/summons), and what 130 Destiny Stars really buy is in *Destiny Star odds* on the [Blog](/blog).

### Bleed and Burn rebalance

First announced on 16 February:

| | Damage per tick, before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Rare | 4x–6x ATK | 1.5x–3x ATK |
| Epic | 5x–8x ATK | 2x–4x ATK |
| Legendary | 5x–10x ATK | 2x–5x ATK |
| Cap per tick | about 4–5% of max HP | 1.5% of max HP |

Skills that hit and apply Bleed or Burn also lost about half their damage, and some bosses' immunities changed. ![Kinslayer Sodama](hero:) was hit hardest; their numbers are below. Players at level 25 or higher got a free pack of Destiny Stars, Gems and Codexes to make up for it.

The update also brought stronger Awakening bonuses, balance changes for every boss, Mass Invoker Dismissal, 13 new Invokers (6 Legendaries) and 2 Titans.
