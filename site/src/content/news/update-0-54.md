## What happened

- **Spires Auto-Climb:** spend Keys and climb automatically, for free.
- **Quests and logins:** Bonus Quests, more Progress Quests, Weekly and Monthly Quests that adapt to you, and longer Daily Login Rewards.
- ![Destiny Star](item:) **One-time Legendary on your 50th Destiny Star.**
- **Stronger Awakening bonuses**, balance changes for every boss, and Mass Invoker Dismissal.
- **13 new Invokers** (6 Legendaries) and 2 new Titans.

**Bleed and Burn rebalance**, first announced on 16 February:

| | Damage per tick, before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Rare | 4x–6x ATK | 1.5x–3x ATK |
| Epic | 5x–8x ATK | 2x–4x ATK |
| Legendary | 5x–10x ATK | 2x–5x ATK |
| Cap per tick | about 4–5% of max HP | 1.5% of max HP |

Skills that hit and apply Bleed or Burn also lost about half their damage, and some bosses' immunities changed. Players at level 25 or higher got a free pack of Destiny Stars, Gems and Codexes.

**13 March: a bonus Legendary for existing players.** Anyone who had summoned at least 50 Destiny Stars before 0.54 could claim a free Legendary from the ![Divine Star](item:) Divine Star summon screen. Players under 190 Destiny Stars had their counter moved on by 50.
