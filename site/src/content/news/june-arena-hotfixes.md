**Three hotfixes on 16 and 17 June 2026 made the Arena Bazaar cheaper, eased Update 0.58's control limits and fixed Koltmos.**

### Arena Bazaar prices

| Item | Limit | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![Rare Codex](item:) | Daily, unchanged | 2,000 | 1,000 |
| ![Rare Prism](item:) | Weekly, 3 → 5 | 2,500 | 1,500 |
| ![Epic Codex](item:) | Weekly, 3 → 5 | 5,000 | 3,000 |
| ![Epic Prism](item:) | Weekly, 3 → 5 | 6,000 | 3,500 |

At Silver IV a win pays 140 Coins (see [Update 0.56](/news/update-0-56)), so a Rare Codex now takes 8 wins and all five Epic Codexes a week take 108.

### Control

Update 0.58's Arena modifiers had hit some Invokers too hard, so their control lasts longer at max level: Durlag the Kingsbane, Netherweb Sildrea, Shredripper Matescu (Sagastorm up to 7s), Lektrine the Storm Witch, Kyoze the Testament (Taunt up to 7s), Gondo The Watchman and Ballus the Bull. Vakia, Nyctra and Veyna now aim for back-line Supports in the Arena.

### Koltmos deals Blight

Koltmos's Skills had been dealing Bloodrot by mistake. Every Invoker built for him already had Blight Tolerance, so bring Blight with Manashock. See the [Koltmos guide](/bosses/koltmos) and *Boss corruptions and tolerance sets* on the [Blog](/blog).
