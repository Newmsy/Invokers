## What happened

**16 June: Arena Bazaar prices**

| Item | Limit | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![Rare Codex](item:) | Daily, unchanged | 2,000 | 1,000 |
| ![Rare Prism](item:) | Weekly, 3 → 5 | 2,500 | 1,500 |
| ![Epic Codex](item:) | Weekly, 3 → 5 | 5,000 | 3,000 |
| ![Epic Prism](item:) | Weekly, 3 → 5 | 6,000 | 3,500 |

**16 June: control after Update 0.58's Arena modifiers.** Longer control at max level for Durlag the Kingsbane, Netherweb Sildrea, Shredripper Matescu (Sagastorm up to 7s), Lektrine the Storm Witch, Kyoze the Testament (Taunt up to 7s), Gondo The Watchman and Ballus the Bull. Vakia, Nyctra and Veyna now aim for back-line Supports in the Arena.

**17 June: Koltmos deals Blight.** His Skills had been dealing Bloodrot by mistake. Every Invoker built for Koltmos already had Blight Tolerance.

## Our take

- **Arena Coins at the new prices:** a Silver IV win pays 140 Coins (see [Update 0.56](/news/update-0-56)), so a Rare Codex takes 8 wins and all five Epic Codexes a week take 108.
- **Bring Blight to Koltmos**, with Manashock. See the [Koltmos guide](/bosses/koltmos) and *Boss corruptions and tolerance sets* on the [Blog](/blog).
