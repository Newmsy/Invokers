**Two Legendaries arrive with Grimnall this spooky season:** a scarecrow Titan, [Hain](/titans/hain/), and a pumpkin-throwing Invoker, ![Wrakk the Harvester](hero:).

### Hain

**Hain is a Legendary Melee Attacker Titan that only Eldrin Invokers can bind.** The bound Invoker gets **+35 Combo SPD**, rising to **+70** at full Empowerment, in every mode. That puts Hain in the top three Recommended Titans for 20 of the 30 Eldrin Invokers, behind Wyvold and Keltaur.

Hain's skills buff only Hain, then cash those buffs in (numbers at max skill level):

- **Skill 1** hits one enemy twice for 270% ATK and gives Hain **50% Crit Rate Up** and **80% Crit DMG Up** for 16s.
- **Skill 2** hits the 10 lowest-HP enemies nearby for 280% ATK, with **+12% Crit DMG per buff** on Hain.
- **The Combo** finishes on the 10 lowest-HP enemies in front, **+8% damage per buff**.
- **The Invokation** hits the 10 lowest-HP enemies for 470% ATK and adds **30% Combo SPD Up**, then **30% Skill Recovery Up** when it ends.

Hain is A tier on Spires, Althar, Sorgott, Hyxxis, Yskara, Koltmos and Omnissi (Astral).

### Wrakk the Harvester

**Wrakk is a Legendary Stonehearts Earth Ranged Attacker, in the Destiny Star summons.** Where Grimnall feeds on buffs, Wrakk spreads debuffs (numbers at max skill level):

- **Skill 1** hits for 300% ATK with **60% RES Down** for 6s.
- **Skill 2** hits 3 times for 205% ATK each, adding **50% ATK Down**, **70% ACC Down** and **30% Skill Recovery Down** for 16s.
- **Skill 3** hits the 10 lowest-HP enemies around a target for 315% ATK with **30% Blind** for 16s, so their Combo Attacks can miss.
- **The Ultimate** hits twice for 524% ATK and **ignores 6% of the target's DEF per buff**.
- **The last Combo hit** extends 2 debuffs on up to 10 enemies by 2s.

Every debuff needs Accuracy, so build Wrakk with ACC first. Wrakk has the 10th-highest ATK of all 60 Legendaries when fully awakened, and its Tolerances are Daze and Atrophy.
