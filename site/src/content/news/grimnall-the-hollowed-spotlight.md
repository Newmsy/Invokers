**Grimnall the Hollowed is the hero of the Grimnall Chase, which starts on Monday 12 October 2026.** Grimnall is a Legendary Nocturna Earth Ranged Attacker, bought for 170 ![](item:Pumpkin) Pumpkins in the Event Shop. See the [chase guide](/news/grimnall-chase/) for where the Pumpkins come from.

### The kit: more buffs, more Mana

Grimnall has no buffs of its own. Every buff your team puts on Grimnall feeds the kit instead (numbers at max skill level):

- **Skill 1** shoots one enemy twice for 145% ATK a hit, **+60% damage** when Grimnall's HP% is higher than the target's.
- **Skill 2** hits one enemy for 610% ATK and gives **35 Ultimate Mana per buff** on Grimnall.
- **Skill 3** hits the 10 lowest-HP enemies for 320% ATK and gives **50 Titan Mana per buff**.
- **The Ultimate** hits one enemy 3 times for 345% ATK, with **75 Titan Mana per buff** on each hit.
- **The last Combo hit** extends 2 random buffs on Grimnall by 2s.

### Where it fits

**Grimnall hits hard but is fragile:** 5th-highest ATK of all 60 Legendaries when fully awakened, but the lowest HP. That makes the Skill 1 bonus easiest against bosses you've already worn down, so pair Grimnall with a buffer and a healer. With 3 buffs up, the Ultimate alone gives 675 Titan Mana. Skill 3 picking the weakest targets makes it a finisher in Arena and on waves. Its Tolerances are Mana Shock and Blight.
