**Invokers came to Windows and Mac on 12 August 2026**, with controller support, key rebinding and +20% VIP Points ([announcement](https://invokers.com/news/53/)). Progress carries over from mobile, but only through a Google email: link it on mobile (Menu → Account) before the first PC login. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center don't carry over, and the PC client would make a new account instead.

**The Web Shop on invokers.com followed on 28 September**, with Google login, local-currency prices and the same +20% VIP Points. That's 120 points per £1 or $1 instead of 100, so VIP 25 costs about £83,700 instead of £100,436. See *VIP levels* on the [Blog](/blog).

![Energy](item:) **The free 50 daily Energy pack moved to the Web Shop on 29 September, then came back.** After player feedback it returned in game on every platform the next day, with a one-off gift of 300 Energy and 20 Multi-Battles. It's worth claiming every day: 100 Energy costs 150 Gems in the Gem Shop, so the pack is worth 75 Gems a day, or **2,250 Gems a month**.
