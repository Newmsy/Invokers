## What happened

- **12 August: desktop client** for Windows and Mac, with controller support, key rebinding and +20% VIP Points. Progress carries over from mobile through a linked Google account. ([Announcement](https://invokers.com/news/53/))
- **28 September: Web Shop** on invokers.com, with Google login, local-currency prices and +20% VIP Points.
- ![Energy](item:) **The free 50 daily Energy pack is now web-only**, from the next day at 00:00 UTC.

## Our take

- **Claim the free Energy every day.** In the Gem Shop, 100 Energy costs 150 Gems, so 50 Energy is worth 75 Gems: **2,250 Gems a month**.
- **+20% VIP Points** is 120 points per £1 or $1 instead of 100. VIP 25 then costs about £83,700 instead of £100,436. See *VIP levels* on the [Blog](/blog).
